  • md3-input-element can be applied to either an <input> or a <textarea>. Textareas automatically grow to fit their content.
  • When using a custom icon, always apply the md3-icon-element directive to the icon element. Leading and trailing icons should follow the Material 3 recommended size of 24dp.
  • A trailing md3-icon-element and a trailing md3-icon-button are mutually exclusive slots; use whichever fits the interaction (e.g. a visibility toggle vs. a clear button).
  • The error state is driven by aria-invalid on the native input, or automatically derived from a bound control (invalid and touched/dirty) or a formControlName.
  • Use md3-supporting-text to display helper or error text below the field.
