Badges

Badges show notifications, counts, or status information on navigation items, icons, and other elements.

API

Name Type Default
small

Renders the badge as a small dot with no visible content, used to indicate that new information is available without a count.

 input<boolean> false
  • md3-badge is a directive, not a positioned element — it does not anchor itself to sibling content. Wrap the badge and its target (e.g. an icon) in a container with position: relative and position the badge absolutely, as shown in the md3-nav-item and md3-menu-item components.
  • Content is projected into the badge, so it can hold a count or a short text label such as NEW. Keep labels to a word or two — the badge grows with its content and has no truncation of its own. Numeric counts should follow the Material guidance of capping large numbers (e.g. 999+).
  • Set small to render a dot indicator and omit any projected content, since the small variant hides its content visually.
