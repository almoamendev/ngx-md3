Snackbars

Snackbars show brief, transient messages at the bottom of the screen. Only one snackbar is shown at a time — additional calls made while one is visible wait their turn in a queue.

  • Only one snackbar is ever visible. SnackbarService.open() queues additional calls (FIFO) unless replaceCurrent is set, which jumps the queue and dismisses whatever's currently showing.
  • The message can be a plain string, or a TemplateRef for content that needs to keep re-rendering — like the progress example above, which reads this page's own signal directly. The surface, action button, close icon, positioning, and animation are always owned by the snackbar shell; there's no arbitrary custom-component API, since that's how a snackbar stops being a snackbar per Material Design 3.
  • The auto-dismiss timer pauses automatically on hover/focus and resumes when the pointer or focus leaves.
  • Set stackedAction: true when the action label is too long to sit comfortably next to the message on one row. The action drops to its own line below the message, aligned to the inline-end — Material Design 3's "long action" layout. It's ignored when there's no actionLabel.
  • onAction attaches a callback to the action button specifically — it never runs for the close icon, which always just dismisses. Use it instead of ref.onAction().subscribe() when you don't need to keep the ref around.
  • Snackbars render full-width at the bottom of the viewport, but never lower than the scaffold's main content area — they sit above a bottom navigation bar automatically when one is present.
  • Use bottomOffset to clear your own fixed bottom elements that the scaffold doesn't account for, like a floating action button. It stacks on top of the automatic bottom-nav inset rather than replacing it.
