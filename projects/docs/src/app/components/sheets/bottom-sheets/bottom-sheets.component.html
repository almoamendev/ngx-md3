Bottom sheets

Bottom sheets are modal panels anchored to the bottom of the viewport. They hold supporting content or actions related to the current screen, the same way a dialog does, but slide up from the bottom edge instead.

  • Bottom sheets are custom components opened through the bottom sheet service, the same way dialogs are opened through the dialog service.
  • The sheet lays your component's content out on a grid with header, body and actions areas. Place your own elements in them with grid-area; the body is the part that scrolls.
  • Dragging the sheet down dismisses it, either by taking it past a bit under half its height or by flinging it. A shorter drag snaps back. The drag handle is the affordance for it, and is where the gesture is most reliable on touch, but the drag starts anywhere on the sheet that is not scrolling content of its own. Set gestures: false to turn it off.
  • A bottom sheet is always modal: it brings its own scrim and closes when the scrim is clicked, when Escape is pressed, or when another bottom sheet opens on top of it.
  • Unlike side sheets, bottom sheets are not part of the page scaffold — they render in an overlay above it, so there is no outlet to register.
