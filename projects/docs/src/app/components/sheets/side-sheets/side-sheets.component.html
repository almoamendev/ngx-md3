Side sheets

Side sheets are panels anchored to the start or end of the layout. They hold supporting content next to the main task — filters, details, a form — without taking the screen over the way a dialog does.

  • Side sheets are custom components opened through the sheets service, the same way dialogs are.
  • When creating your sheet component, you can optionally use md3-side-sheet-header, md3-side-sheet-body and md3-side-sheet-actions. The body is the part that scrolls.
  • Opening a sheet on a side that already has one hides the first sheet and shows it again when the new one closes. Pass closeExisting to replace it instead.
  • A modal sheet brings its own scrim and closes when the scrim is clicked. Default and standard sheets share the layout with the content beside them and stay open.

Outlets

A side sheet renders into an outlet rather than an overlay of its own, which is what lets it push the content beside it instead of covering it. The scaffold registers one outlet per side, so a sheet opened anywhere in the app lands in the current layout.

  • Outlets stack: whichever registered last is where new sheets open. A full screen dialog registers its own pair while it is up, so sheets opened from inside it render in the dialog and are closed with it.
  • Calling openSideSheet() with no registered outlet for that side throws — the layout has to be in place first.
