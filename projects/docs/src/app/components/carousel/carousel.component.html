Carousel

Carousels show a collection of items that can be scrolled on and off the screen. They emphasise visuals, and can carry brief text that adapts as an item changes size.

Items are laid out against a set of keylines — large, medium, small, and an off-screen anchor — and adopt the size of whichever keyline they are passing through. That is what makes items grow into the focal range and crop away as they leave it, rather than simply sliding past.

item-size is a preference, not a fixed width. The carousel adjusts it as little as it can while still producing an arrangement that fills the container exactly — which is why the solved sizes above rarely match the value you set. Resize the window, or change the settings, to watch it re-solve.

Alignment

A start-aligned carousel keeps its large items at the beginning of the container, followed by the medium and small items. A centre-aligned one puts the large items in the middle with smaller items on both sides, so it needs matching keylines either side of the focal range and shifts them at both ends of the list rather than only at the end.

Controlled index

index is a two-way model holding the item leading the focal range. Setting it scrolls the carousel; scrolling the carousel updates it.

It is clamped to lastIndex, which is lower than the last item's index: once the carousel is scrolled to the end, the trailing items all share the focal range, so none of them has a resting position of its own. Bind buttons to atStart and atEnd rather than comparing against the item count, or the last few presses will do nothing.

Reacting to the item's size

Every item carries the size band it currently renders at as a class — md3-large, md3-medium or md3-small — so content can adapt from a stylesheet with no bindings at all. Bands are snapped to the nearest of the arrangement's three sizes and flip at the midpoint between them. The labels in the demo above use exactly this.

For finer control, each item also exposes a maskRatio signal — 0 while the item is fully unmasked, rising towards 1 as it crops away. Use it when a step change between three bands is too coarse and you want content to track the crop continuously.

Accessibility

The carousel is a focusable group with aria-roledescription="carousel", and each item is announced as a slide with its position in the list. When the carousel has focus:

  • Arrow keys move one item at a time, mirrored automatically under right-to-left.
  • Home and End jump to the first and last item.

Focusable content inside a cropped item is scrolled into the focal range automatically, so tabbing never lands on something the reader cannot see. Programmatic scrolling honours prefers-reduced-motion.

Content

Masking works by cropping the item towards its centre while its content stays at full size, so items look best with full-bleed content — an image or a background that reaches the edges. Content with its own padding or margins will reveal empty space as the item crops.
